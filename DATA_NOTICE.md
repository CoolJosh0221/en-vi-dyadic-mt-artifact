# Data and rights notice

The English requests are short excerpts selected from the Enron Email Corpus
through the pinned `corbt/enron-emails` revision cited in the paper. The
artifact assigns new release-local IDs and contains no original record keys,
email addresses, message headers, source spans, or item-level participant data.

The Vietnamese strings are machine-generated outputs. Synthetic workplace
profiles were created for the experiment and do not describe the original
Enron senders or recipients.

Human-derived content is limited to published aggregate and pseudonymous
rater-specific summary statistics under the A1 and A2 labels. No item-level
judgment, free-text response, adjudication rationale, or private audit record
is included.
